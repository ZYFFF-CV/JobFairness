# Stage2 P3 Seed-2019 Early-Stop Audit

## Decision

Status: `decisive_early_fail_for_primary_containment_gate`

The bounded P3 audit completed for the seed-2019 baseline and
`multi_layer_path_gate_full` checkpoints. The primary method cannot satisfy the
frozen Stage2 containment gate because multiple trained target nodes show
positive held-out probe-AUC deltas in seed 2019. The gate requires every target
node to have a negative delta in every seed. Results from seeds 2020 and 2021
therefore cannot restore a full or partial pass.

The remaining 25 checkpoint probes are not approved. Continuing them would
characterize additional failure modes but cannot change the registered method
decision.

The decision is also generated by
`fuxictr_ext/fairjob/stage2_containment_audit.py`; the server-side JSON is kept
at `stage2/reports/p3_seed2019_early_stop.json` under the external workdir.

| Joint path | Row-disjoint delta | User-disjoint delta |

| cross_block_increments | +0.004272 | +0.024630 |
| branch_outputs_with_fusion | -0.001546 | +0.027707 |

The user-disjoint joint paths all show material amplification. Sentinel
`embedding_flat` also increases by `+0.018416` row-disjoint and `+0.025836`
user-disjoint. This is representation migration/amplification rather than
graph-wide containment.

Some independent tree probes decrease on selected nodes. Those decreases do
not override the result because the frozen protocol uses the maximum held-out
AUC across the complete probe family. Selecting only the favorable probe after
reading test results would violate the preregistered rule.

## Scientific Interpretation

The Stage2 multi-layer multi-path containment hypothesis fails its frozen
screening criterion for the primary method. The result supports a narrower
negative finding: adversarial pressure can reduce decodability on one DNN path
while redistributing or amplifying decodability through cross and joint paths,
including for unseen raw users.

No second-round weight search, threshold relaxation, five-seed expansion, or
new backbone is justified under the registered stopping rule. Any future work
must begin as a new hypothesis and protocol rather than as a repair of this
pilot.
